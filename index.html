Post craft

Each post follows the captivate, fascinate, educate, offer formula. Posts stay short. Not every post needs an offer.

Captivate
A hook in the first line.
Fascinate
One surprising fact or turn.
Educate
One idea only.
Offer
A soft call to action on about 1 in 3 Thaddeus posts and on selected Magis posts. Courtney's posts carry none.

Frequently Asked Questions

Six short answers.

How do I move around this playbook?

Use the menu on the left. It lists every section in order: the idea, who we talk to, three voices, the 12 weeks, post craft, approval, measurement, action plan, decisions and this FAQ. Press Take the Tour to replay the tour.

What are Threads A, B and C?

They are the three themes every post belongs to. Thread A asks what is working. Thread B is less typing, more court. Thread C is behind the bench, told by Courtney.

Who are the reader types, and are the organizations real targets?

Each reader type shows the job titles and sample organizations that readers come from. They are examples that make each reader specific. They are not a target list.

How do I read the 12 weeks?

Each week shows up to three posts, one per channel. Press a card to see the hook example and who it speaks to. Use the channel and reader filters to see one voice or one reader. Magis reposts the founders' posts most weeks and posts on its own about 1 to 2 times every two weeks.

How do I confirm something or suggest a change?

On a reader type, press a Draft tag to confirm the field or suggest a change. To comment on any text, highlight it and add your comment. Open the comments panel and send all comments when you finish. Geri receives each one with the exact text you highlighted.

Who approves the posts?

Thaddeus approves his own posts and the Magis posts. Courtney approves hers. Jason is not part of approval.

Who is commenting?

Your name goes with your comments so Geri knows who they are from.
